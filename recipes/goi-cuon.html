Vietnamese Spring Rolls

Description

Vietnamese spring rolls are fresh, light rolls made with soft rice paper filled with shrimp, rice noodles, lettuce, and herbs. They're usually served cold with a tasty dipping sauce like peanut or hoisin, making them perfect as a snack or appetizer.

  • Rice paper
  • Cooked shrimp and pork
  • Rice noodles
  • Lettuce
  • Mint or basil
  • Carrot slices
  • Peanut or hoisin dipping sauce
  1. Dip rice paper in warm water to soften.
  2. Add shrimp, noodles, and veggies in the center.
  3. Roll it up lightly.
  4. Serve with dipping sauce.
